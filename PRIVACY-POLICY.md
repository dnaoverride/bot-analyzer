# BotAnalyzer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-02

BotAnalyzer processes social media evidence **locally on your device**. This policy describes what the application accesses and stores.

## What we process

When you use BotAnalyzer, the application may store and process:

- Public profile URLs and handles you enter or import
- Post URLs and comment text you provide
- Optional cropped profile images (converted to local hashes and small previews)
- Your review decisions (pending, include, dismiss)
- Case titles and manual submission status notes you enter

## Where data is stored

- **Standalone dashboard:** browser `localStorage` on your machine
- **Chrome extension:** `chrome.storage.local` on your device
- **Exports:** JSON/CSV/TXT files you choose to download

There is no BotAnalyzer-operated cloud database, analytics endpoint, or remote model download in this version.

## Extension permissions

- **storage:** save your cases, evidence, and settings locally
- **activeTab / scripting (developer build only):** read currently visible TikTok comments only after you click the extension icon. The Chrome Web Store manual build does not include automated collection.

## What we do not do

- No automatic mass reporting to social networks
- No collection of passwords, cookies, private messages, or hidden account data
- No facial recognition or biometric identity matching
- No sale of user data

## Sharing files with others

You may export a **shareable case** JSON file and send it to another person. That file contains only the public evidence fields you export. It does not include your review decisions or submission status unless you separately share a personal report package.

You are responsible for lawful collection, redaction, and sharing of evidence in your jurisdiction and under platform terms.

## Deletion

Use **Evidencija → Obriši lokalnu evidenciju** to remove local data. Previously downloaded files remain on your device until you delete them.
